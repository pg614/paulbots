The top 3 things costing you jobs

1
There's no way to book online. Your Google listing only offers "Call now". Someone searching at 9 PM who doesn't want to call has no next step, so they may try the next company.
2
Missed calls have no safety net. You promise 24/7 service. Any call that rings out or hits voicemail is a customer who may dial a competitor. An instant text-back keeps them with you.
3
Your mobile site is slow, and reviews go unanswered. The main content takes about 14 seconds to appear on a phone (score 44/100), and none of your newest ~20 reviews has a reply. Both are easy wins.
