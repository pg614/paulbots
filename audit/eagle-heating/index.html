The top 3 things costing you jobs

1
After-hours callers get voicemail. Your contact page asks after-hours emergency callers to leave a message. Someone with no heat at 10 PM often calls the next company instead of waiting. An AI receptionist answers every call, books it, or alerts your on-call tech.
2
Booking means waiting up to a business day. There's no Book button on Google, and the website's appointment form promises a reply within 24 hours, Monday–Friday. Online booking lets customers pick a time on the spot.
3
Review replies have stopped recently. You replied to 7 of your newest 10 Google reviews, which is a good habit, but the last reply was about 4 months ago. Picking it back up shows new customers someone is listening.
