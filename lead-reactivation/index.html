Questions

How are reach, channel, and setup decided?

We agree the reach, the channel, and the setup with you before launch. The campaign is built around your list, your goals, and your permission to contact those people.

Can every old lead be contacted?

A past inquiry does not by itself mean every person should be contacted again. We confirm permission, leave out anyone who asked not to be contacted, and include a simple opt-out. You review that plan with us.

When does outreach start?

After you review and approve the campaign. This form starts the conversation. Please leave customer lists and sensitive personal details out of it.

Is this the same as finding new prospects?

Reactivation is for people who already inquired, quoted, or bought, and only where you may contact them. Reaching people who never contacted you is prospecting, a separate service.
