Lead reactivation

Have thousands of old leads?

Let’s scope a reactivation campaign. How many people can be contacted, how many messages or calls can run at once, which channel is used, and which model is used all depend on your list, your permission to contact those people, and a review of the setup. We are not stating a tested volume, a supported channel, or a named model here.

Modern AI can help draft and sort conversations. What actually runs is decided when the campaign is scoped, not by this page.

List review, then outreach, then a handoff

  • Review the list, the permission, and the suppressions. Take out people who asked not to be contacted. A prior inquiry alone does not make every channel acceptable.
  • Tailor the outreach. An unfinished quote, an old inquiry, and a past customer are not the same note. You read the messages before they are used.
  • Sort the replies. People who respond can be qualified by what they need.
  • Hand the conversation to you. Your team takes it from there. Appointments and revenue are not guaranteed.

You review the campaign before launch

Nothing is sent from this website. There is no list upload. A person reviews the plan with you, including a simple way for someone to opt out. We do not certify that a campaign meets every law. Confirm the permission you have before anyone is contacted.

What this is not

  • Not cold outreach. People who never contacted you belong with the Prospecting Bot.
  • Not the free 30-day website lead bot.
  • Not a priced package. No reactivation price is listed here. Other custom bot plans start at only $299/month and are scoped separately. See pricing.
  • Not a substitute for deciding whether reactivation is the right first step. A free AI assessment looks across the business from what you share.

Questions

Can every old lead be contacted?

No. A past inquiry does not by itself allow a call, text, or email on every channel. We confirm the permission you have, suppress do-not-contact records, and plan a simple opt-out. A person reviews that plan. This is not a legal-compliance guarantee.

Will you send the messages when I submit the form?

No. The form only asks us to discuss the idea. You review the campaign before anything is sent. Do not paste a customer list into the form.

Is this the same as finding brand-new prospects?

No. Reactivation is for people who already inquired, quoted, or bought, and only where you may contact them. Reaching people who never contacted you is the Prospecting Bot, and it is a separate service.

What does reactivation cost?

This page does not list a price. The campaign is scoped with you. Paid custom bots, which are a different service, start at only $299/month.

Do you guarantee appointments or revenue?

No. Replies can be qualified and handed to you. Results are not promised.

Every campaign is scoped and reviewed with you before it goes out. Features depend on your list, your permission to contact people, and the tools you use.
