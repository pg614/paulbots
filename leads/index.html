Capture leads who are already reaching out

These are people on your website, people who call, and people who chat. They already took a step toward you. Capturing that inquiry is different from searching for strangers.

  • Website lead bot: greets visitors, answers from information you approve, and collects contact details for your team. This is the free 30-day trial. Examples: forklift dealers, pallet racking, and security alarms.
  • Sales Bot: helps people who are already asking questions move toward a consultation. Sales Bot.
  • Phone and chat answering: the Service Bot takes the name, number, and reason for the call so the inquiry is not lost. That supports lead capture. Industry entry points: HVAC, plumbers, dentists, med spas, law firms, and Columbus, OH.

Keep new conversations going

The Follow-Up Bot contacts new leads with messages you approve. We set it up and test it with you before it goes to work. What it can send depends on your tools. We do not describe a specific response time, and we do not claim a live campaign you can switch on today.

Reconnect with past prospects, where permission exists

Some businesses want to contact people who inquired before and did not buy. That is custom scoping. It depends on whether you have permission to contact them, what you know about them, and the tools you use. We do not run that outreach until we scope it with you. If you are unsure about consent, start with a consultation and your privacy obligations, not a blast. Custom AI Bot · Discuss lead reactivation · Contact.

Generate net-new leads

The Prospecting Bot helps reach and qualify people who have not contacted you, using outreach you approve. You decide who gets contacted. That is lead generation. It is not the same service as capturing the inquiries you already receive, and it is not included in the website trial.

Frequently asked questions

What is included in the free 30-day trial?

Can you reconnect us with past prospects?

That is custom scoping, not a ready-made campaign. It depends on whether you have permission to contact those people and on the tools you use. We set it up with you and test it before it goes to work.
