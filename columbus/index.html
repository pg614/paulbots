A two-minute check

Use this as a simple self-check, not a score, and not a promise of results.

  • When a new inquiry comes in while you are on a job, how fast does someone get back to them?
  • Do you have a list of past customers or old leads you are allowed to contact, and has anyone actually followed up with them?
  • How many Google reviews do you have compared with how long you have been in business?
  • Does your Google Business Profile get replies when people write in?
  • If someone finds you after hours, do they reach a person, a recording, or nothing?
  • Is your website clear and current, or is it something you have been meaning to fix?

If a few of those are unresolved, the free assessment is the right next step. We will look at the whole business and tell you where AI fits, and where it does not.
